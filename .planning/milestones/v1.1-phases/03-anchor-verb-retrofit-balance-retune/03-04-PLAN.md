    **The verb reading for this island.** Dare unstops your ears — the transgression is hearing what kills lesser men, and it pulls the ship toward the reef.     **The reef.** Replace the retired peril-track accounting with favor: each listen bleeds the crew's standing (the acting player's own favor, so the transgressor pays), and the worst face is the wreck risk — its penalty must stay in `alwaysD` so an empty hold cannot silently void it, exactly as the current cells document (that invariant is correct and stays). Rewrite `sirens.onDepart` to read `crewFavor()` against `doomFloor()` — the ship grinds onto the reef when the crew's standing has sunk past the floor — using the same never-take-the-last-sailor cap `helios.onDepart` uses, and keep the alternative branch where the song fades astern. Retire `CONFIG.sirens`' peril-denominated keys in favour of favor-denominated ones with the same names' intent.

    **Escalation (D-10).** Tiers 0/1/2 across `The First Notes` → `The Full Song` → `The Reef`: the pull, the payoff and the cost all rise. Keep the worst face as the wreck locus at every tier, escalating.
