<objective>
Retrofit the first two anchors — Hades and Phaeacia — onto the two-verb grammar without regressing the bespoke mechanics that make them work.

Purpose: PITFALLS.md Pitfall 9 warns that this is the step most likely to be mistaken for content authoring when it is really an engine extension: `runHades` and `runPhaeacia` never call the act path at all today, and Phaeacia has no player choice whatsoever. The retrofit must ADD a verb scene that the existing revival/peek and gift-court mechanics keep living underneath, not replace them.

Output: two anchors that play like the islands — commit blind, throw, reveal, read the beat — while their validated peek, revival economy and favor-weighted gifts-only court remain exactly as proven.
</objective>

- **Restructured `runPhaeacia()`** — welcome (hold refill) → NEW court verb scene → the song (favor-weighted, gifts-only) → gift-ride.
- **New/extended CONFIG:** `CONFIG.hades` (the extra-glimpse and rites magnitudes) and additional `CONFIG.phaeacia` keys for the court scene's gift magnitudes.
