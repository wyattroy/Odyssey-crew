<objective>
Close the phase: tune the economy against the fixed multi-seed sweep until the intended tension actually shows in the distribution, then make the project's own documentation tell the truth about the game it now describes.

Purpose: the measured pre-redesign baseline was a death spiral — half of all seeds ended with the whole crew dead and no run ever brought more than one sailor home. Doing it last is deliberate: per-scene tuning in isolation is exactly how favor becomes dominated or trivially abundant (PITFALLS Pitfall 8), so the cross-episode reconciliation happens once, after every payoff exists.

Output: a tuned economy that clears the D-09 acceptance bar in assert mode, a recorded tuning log, and a PROJECT.md that matches the shipped game.
</objective>

  <action>
    Tune the numbers, and only the numbers, until the distribution matches the design target.

    **The target (D-09), restated as the shape the sweep must show.** The greedy/dare road survives but wins little favor. The pious/abide road accumulates favor but is fragile — cooperate and you win, defect and you merely live. With revival reachable from every beat, MOST crew reach Ithaca unless the crew bankrupts its favor. Favor stays contested: winner totals spread widely across seeds rather than converging. Every run terminates at a winner and nothing ever dead-ends. And the hold economy stays under REAL pressure — a run where nobody ever risks starving has over-corrected just as badly as the original spiral.

    **The method.** Work the loop: read the current sweep report, form one hypothesis about which CONFIG magnitude is responsible, change that magnitude, re-run, compare. Change ONE lever at a time and record each move and its measured effect — a tuning log of "what I moved, what it did" is the deliverable that makes the next milestone's automated pass possible. Start from the named diagnosis 03-04 recorded rather than exploring blind. Use a large seed count (at least 80) for every decision so you are reading a distribution rather than a run, and re-check the small-N seeds only for transcript reading.

    **The levers, in rough order of leverage.** `CONFIG.econ.abideHold` versus `CONFIG.econ.dareStash` sets whether the commons or the satchel is the better place to stand — this is the master dial for the cooperate/defect split. `CONFIG.econ.abideFavor` versus `CONFIG.econ.dareCaught` sets how fast favor accumulates against how fast it bleeds, which sets both the win-condition spread and how affordable revival is. `CONFIG.charon.toll` sets how many lives the crew's favor can buy — the single strongest lever on the all-dead rate and on whether favor stays scarce. `CONFIG.divine` thresholds set how punishing the seas and the collective dooms are. `CONFIG.crossing` sets leg length and therefore total meals demanded. `CONFIG.ithaca` floors and pot set whether the finale discriminates between players. `CONFIG.bot` temperament knobs are a LAST resort — retuning bot behaviour to hit a target hides an economy problem rather than fixing it, so change them only to correct a bot that plays its temperament wrongly, never to move a metric.

    **Guardrails.** Do not edit any beats prose, any scene structure, any engine function, or any `econD` call site — if a target seems unreachable without a structural change, stop and record that in the SUMMARY as a finding rather than reaching for the structure. Do not weaken a `TARGETS` value to make the gate pass; if a target is genuinely wrong (as opposed to unmet), say so explicitly in the SUMMARY with the reasoning and the evidence, and change it deliberately. Keep every number in CONFIG — a tuning move that introduces a literal anywhere else fails the retunability constraint the whole phase was built around.

    **Finish by locking the bar.** Once `--assert` passes at 80 seeds, confirm it also passes at a larger count (at least 200) so the result is a property of the economy and not of the seed set, and leave the final `TARGETS` block in the file as the shipped acceptance bar for future regressions. Per BALANCE-01, BALANCE-02, BALANCE-03, D-09.
