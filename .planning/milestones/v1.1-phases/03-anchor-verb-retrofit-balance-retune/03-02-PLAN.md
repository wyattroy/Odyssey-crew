<objective>
Turn favor into the crew's lifeline and make keeping the crew whole visibly worth it — without ever creating a dead-end.

Purpose: the tracer proved the new economy resolves; this plan makes it survivable. Today death is a one-way starvation spiral that only Hades can reverse, which is precisely the failure the measured baseline shows (~50% of seeds end with the whole crew dead). Generalising revival moves the permanent-loss condition from starvation to favor bankruptcy, which is a *choice* the crew makes rather than an accident of the marble bag.

Output: a voyage where the dead can buy their way back on their own turn or be bought back by a mate, where a full crew sails shorter legs, and where the only way to lose everyone is to spend the crew's standing with the gods down to nothing.
</objective>

<artifacts_this_phase_produces>
New in `index.html`:
- **New function:** `favorRevive(dead, payer)` — the single place a favor toll buys a life back (payer may be the dead player themselves or a living crewmate); logs, resets the returned player's satchel, and returns whether the toll was paid.
- **Generalised function:** `revivalRound()` — no longer Hades-only; callable from any beat, a no-op when nobody is dead.
- **New call sites:** the top of every island scene (`runIsland`'s scene loop) and every sea leg (`runCrossing`'s loop), plus the existing Hades call.
- **Rewritten:** `deadEndCheck()` — favor bankruptcy as the single permanent end, always resolving to a verdict.
- **New CONFIG keys:** `CONFIG.charon` (`toll`, and any at-Hades modifier), `CONFIG.crossing.fullCrewAt` / `CONFIG.crossing.fullCrewWhiteBonus`.
- **Retired:** the standalone auto-Charon branch inside the old `deadEndCheck()` (superseded by the generalised revival round).
</artifacts_this_phase_produces>
