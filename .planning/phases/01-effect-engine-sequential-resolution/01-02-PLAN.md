<objective>
Change resolution semantics from a simultaneous feasibility check to sequential, turn-ordered resolution against the shared hold. Blind commits STAY simultaneous (collectCommits is untouched), but on reveal the hold is spent one player at a time in a fixed, documented order: the earlier player gets the last ration, the later player is denied and skipped (never partially applied) with narration. The ordering lives behind one isolated `resolutionOrder()` seam so a future favor-weighted / turn-chip scheme can replace it without touching resolution logic.

Purpose: This is the mechanic that makes the commons tension bite the way the design intends — a shared-resource shortfall is decided by who acts first, not by a lot-cast averaging. It is also the intended replacement for the death-spiral-adjacent eat lot-casting, and it establishes the "never partially applied" guarantee the effect engine relies on.

Output: New symbol `resolutionOrder(actors)`; rewritten eat-phase hold resolution; a feasibility gate in the Act reveal path; retirement of the `pickLowest` lot-cast for the eat shortfall. No new files, no dependencies, still one self-contained index.html.
</objective>
