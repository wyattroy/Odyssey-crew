<objective>
Land the declarative effect+narration engine: a `beats` data structure co-located on scene objects (verb x face -> {d, tell}), ONE generic resolver (`resolveEffect`) that consumes it to apply effects and produce narration, a centralized `narrate` formatter, a coverage `validateBeats` validator that fails loud on missing/dead cells, and a single labelled CONFIG payoff convention. Proven end-to-end by converting one already-worked Helios scene onto the new path (the tracer) and then a second, stateful worked scene to prove the resolver generalizes.

Purpose: This is the foundation the whole milestone stands on — it replaces the inline `reskin` delta-mutation + hand-written strings (which conflate compute, mutate, and narrate) with data the designer edits per-cell, and it is the exact seam Phase 2 authoring and Phase 4's board both consume. Locking the engine before content or visuals means a payoff/beat edit never re-touches JS control flow.

Output: New symbols in index.html — `resolveEffect`, `narrate`, `validateBeats`, the `beats` scene field on the converted scenes, and labelled CONFIG payoff constants. No new files, no dependencies, still one self-contained index.html.
</objective>
