<objective>
Expand the tracer board with the crossing strip (UI-SPEC §Layout item 2, §Build order 2): render the marble bag as actual blue/white marbles that visibly drain as they are drawn, and show the live bone roll as a die face during the act reveal — both as pure projections of already-decided reducer values.

Purpose: These are the two elements the user explicitly asked for ("shows the actual blue and white marbles left in the bag; shows the dice and their roll"). They are also the sharpest determinism/masking traps in the phase (Pitfalls 4 and 5): the bag must drain from state (never track its own count), the die must display an already-rolled value (never generate one in a handler), and the dice must be reveal-gated (read p._boneShow, not p.lastBone) so they cannot leak a roll before that player's reveal.

Output: A board that, mid-crossing, shows real draining marbles, and during an act shows the live rolled die face one player at a time — with the 0-human seeded run still completing identically.
</objective>
