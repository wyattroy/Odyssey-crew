<objective>
Close Phase 4 (UI-SPEC §Layout item 6, §Build order 4-5): enforce blind-commit masking across the whole board (public vs private-until-reveal zones), demote the ship's log to a collapsible panel while keeping the transcript intact, and run the final determinism / masking / regression pass.

Purpose: The board's natural pull is to "show everything for spectacle" — which is exactly how blind-commit masking leaks (Pitfall 2), since bot commits already sit in `state.players[i].commit` the instant `collectCommits` starts and all bones are pre-rolled before the reveal loop. This plan makes the public/private split explicit, verifies it under the only condition that exercises masking (2+ humans, director off), preserves the greppable transcript that makes seeded runs debuggable (Pitfall 3), and confirms `?seed=` determinism survived the whole phase.

Output: A board that never leaks a pre-reveal commit or roll, with the log demoted-but-alive, and a proven-deterministic full voyage.
</objective>
