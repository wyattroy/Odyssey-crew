<objective>
Retrofit the finale: Ithaca's three-scene reversal onto the two-verb grammar, with a real reward for bringing the crew home — and a hard guarantee that the hall always resolves to a winner.

Purpose: Ithaca is the only beat that can end the game, so it is the one place a retrofit bug becomes an unresolvable run rather than a bad payoff. It is also where the third verb's last engine remnant lives (the finale's single-purpose patience prompt), so this plan completes "exactly two verbs everywhere". And it carries the finale half of the keep-the-crew-whole incentive that 03-02 deliberately left here.

Output: a finale that plays like the rest of the game, rewards a whole crew, and cannot be made unresolvable by any combination of deaths, bankruptcies or refusals.
</objective>
