## Area 4 — The two-verb pivot (user proposal)
User: Abide should refill the **hold** (not your stash) — xenia = trusting the common good. Then asked whether **Give is now redundant** and could fold into Abide (low rolls → hold, high rolls → favor).
- Claude's take: yes — once Abide fills the hold, Give overlaps it. Proposed the **"die = gods' gaze"** two-verb model (Abide: always hold + high-roll favor; Dare: high-roll unseen stash / low-roll caught −favor). Flagged the tradeoffs (core identity pivot, re-authors Phase 2, updates board/engine, loses the deliberate-sacrifice moment — now carried by favor-revival).
- **Decision:** **Yes — fold to two verbs (Abide/Dare).**
